Appendix B: Market Sizing Methodology
This appendix transforms vague market opportunity claims into rigorous, defensible calculations. The €15 billion TAM isn't just a large number - it's built from both top-down macroeconomic analysis and bottom-up SME population data, with multiple validation methods confirming the size. The progression from TAM to SAM to SOM shows investors exactly how you'll capture value, with specific assumptions they can pressure-test. The inclusion of sensitivity analysis and multiple scenarios demonstrates sophisticated thinking about risk and opportunity.
