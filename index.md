# Appendix B: Market Sizing Methodology

## Executive Summary of Market Opportunity

The market opportunity for RootRise represents a convergence of three massive trends in the MENA region: the SME financing gap, the digital transformation imperative, and the rise of AI-enabled services. Our analysis reveals a Total Addressable Market of €15 billion, with a Serviceable Addressable Market of €2.5 billion that RootRise can realistically target within its core competencies. Based on current growth trajectories and competitive dynamics, we project a Serviceable Obtainable Market of €500 million within five years.

## 1. Total Addressable Market (TAM): €15 Billion

### 1.1 Market Definition and Boundaries

The Total Addressable Market represents the complete revenue opportunity if RootRise could serve every potential SME in the MENA region requiring transformation and investment readiness services. This calculation considers both traditional consulting services and digital transformation initiatives.

### 1.2 Primary Market Sizing Approach: Top-Down Analysis

**Starting with Macroeconomic Data:**

The MENA region's GDP stands at approximately $3.5 trillion as of 2024. SMEs contribute between 20-40% of GDP depending on the country, with an average of 30%. This means SMEs generate approximately $1.05 trillion in economic value.

Research from the World Bank and IFC indicates that SMEs in emerging markets typically invest 1.5-2% of their revenue in consulting and transformation services when accessible. However, due to current market failures, actual spending is only 0.3-0.5% of revenue.

**Calculation Framework:**
- MENA GDP: $3.5 trillion
- SME contribution to GDP: 30% = $1.05 trillion
- Potential spending on transformation (2% of revenue): $21 billion
- Current actual spending (0.5% of revenue): $5.25 billion
- Unmet demand (gap): $15.75 billion

**Converting to Euros:** $21 billion potential market = €19.5 billion
Adjusting for RootRise's serviceable segments (excluding micro-enterprises and large corporates): €15 billion TAM

### 1.3 Validation Through Bottom-Up Analysis

**SME Population Method:**

The MENA region contains approximately 23 million SMEs according to the latest OECD and World Bank data. Breaking these down by size and transformation readiness:

- Micro-enterprises (1-9 employees): 19 million (not target market)
- Small enterprises (10-49 employees): 3.2 million
- Medium enterprises (50-250 employees): 800,000

RootRise's target market focuses on the 4 million small and medium enterprises that have growth potential and need for transformation services.

**Service Penetration Assumptions:**
- 30% of small enterprises could benefit from transformation: 960,000
- 60% of medium enterprises need transformation: 480,000
- Total addressable enterprises: 1.44 million

**Average Revenue Potential:**
- Small enterprises: €8,000 annual service value
- Medium enterprises: €25,000 annual service value
- Weighted average: €12,000 per enterprise

**Bottom-up TAM Calculation:**
1.44 million enterprises × €12,000 = €17.28 billion

This bottom-up validation of €17.28 billion aligns closely with our top-down estimate of €15 billion, providing confidence in the TAM assessment.

### 1.4 Market Growth Factors

The TAM is expanding due to several structural factors:

**Digital Transformation Acceleration:** McKinsey research shows the SME digitization market in Europe alone grew from €84 billion in 2021 to over €100 billion by 2024, representing 20% annual growth. MENA markets typically lag by 3-5 years but grow faster once adoption begins.

**Increased Capital Availability:** Development finance institutions have committed over $50 billion to MENA SME development through 2030, requiring investment-ready pipeline development.

**Regulatory Drivers:** Vision 2030 initiatives across Gulf states mandate SME development, creating policy-driven demand for transformation services.

**AI Cost Reduction:** AI-enabled service delivery reduces costs by 60-80%, making previously inaccessible services affordable to mid-market SMEs.

## 2. Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM): €2.5 Billion

### 2.1 Geographic Focus Refinement

While the TAM covers all MENA markets, RootRise's initial serviceable market focuses on accessible geographies with favorable operating conditions:

**Tier 1 Markets (Immediate Focus):**
- Egypt: 3.4 million SMEs, €400 million market
- UAE: 350,000 SMEs, €300 million market
- Saudi Arabia: 950,000 SMEs, €450 million market
- Jordan: 160,000 SMEs, €120 million market

**Tier 2 Markets (Year 2-3 Expansion):**
- Morocco: 2.8 million SMEs, €350 million market
- Tunisia: 680,000 SMEs, €180 million market
- Lebanon: 145,000 SMEs, €100 million market
- Qatar/Bahrain/Kuwait: 120,000 SMEs, €150 million market

**Tier 3 Markets (Future Consideration):**
- Iraq, Libya, Syria, Yemen: Currently excluded due to operational challenges

### 2.2 Sectoral Prioritization

RootRise's methodology works best for specific sectors that form our serviceable market:

**Primary Sectors (70% of SAM):**
- Manufacturing: 25% of target SMEs
- Agri-food and processing: 20%
- Logistics and distribution: 15%
- Professional services: 10%

**Secondary Sectors (20% of SAM):**
- Retail and wholesale trade: 10%
- Construction and real estate: 5%
- Healthcare services: 5%

**Excluded Sectors (10% of TAM):**
- Pure technology startups (served by accelerators)
- Financial services (regulatory complexity)
- Oil and gas (enterprise focus)

### 2.3 Investment Readiness Filter

Not all SMEs seeking transformation are investment-ready or seeking capital:

**Investment Intention Analysis:**
- 35% of SMEs actively seeking growth capital
- 25% considering capital in next 2 years
- 40% focused on operational improvement only

RootRise's investment readiness focus means we primarily serve the 60% seeking or considering capital, reducing our serviceable market accordingly.

### 2.4 SAM Calculation

Starting with TAM of €15 billion:
- Geographic focus (Tier 1 & 2 markets): 65% = €9.75 billion
- Sectoral focus (90% of sectors): 90% = €8.78 billion
- Investment readiness filter (60%): 60% = €5.27 billion
- Competitive accessibility (50% not locked into existing solutions): 50% = €2.63 billion

**Final SAM: €2.5 billion** (rounded for conservative estimation)
